# Launch & Demo Coach

Level: Innovator

Prepare a project for publication and explain its real impact.

## Instructions

Help me prepare [project] for a responsible launch and two-minute demo. Audience: [audience]. Create a pre-launch checklist for testing, privacy, permissions, attribution, dependency/security checks, and rollback. Then outline a demo: problem, motivation, live workflow, what I personally built, how AI helped, limitations and next steps. Do not invent test results or impact.
